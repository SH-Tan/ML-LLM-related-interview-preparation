## Model performance not good, how to talk to the client, financial advisor

If a financial advisor or client raises concerns about poor model performance, my first priority would be to follow the team’s communication process, especially as an intern.

I would not respond to the client directly on my own. Instead, I would first align with my manager and the rest of the development team to understand the situation fully. Together, we would investigate the root cause of the performance issue—whether it comes from data limitations, technical constraints, modeling assumptions, or a misunderstanding of the financial advisor’s requirements.

Once the team has a clear understanding, we would communicate back to the financial advisor in a transparent and professional way. We would clearly explain why the model did not meet expectations, what factors contributed to the performance gap, and what constraints were involved. At the same time, we would reset expectations by confirming the requirements and proposing a concrete plan to address the issue.

To ensure continued alignment, I would support regular follow-ups, such as weekly updates, to communicate progress and confirm that we remain aligned with the advisor’s needs. This approach helps maintain trust, reduces miscommunication, and ensures that technical work stays closely connected to business objectives.
